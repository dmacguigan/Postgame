1. League

Find the league ID after leagueId= in your league's URL at fantasy.espn.com. Public leagues need nothing else. Private leagues need two cookies so the app can read the league as you.

How to get espn_s2 and SWID for a private league
  1. Log in at espn.com in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox and open your league page.
  2. Open developer tools: press F12, or right-click the page and choose Inspect.
  3. Chrome and Edge: click the Application tab. Firefox: click the Storage tab.
  4. In the left sidebar expand Cookies and click https://fantasy.espn.com (or any espn.com entry).
  5. Find the row named espn_s2. Double-click its Value, copy the whole long string, paste it below.
  6. Find the row named SWID. Copy its value including the curly braces, like {1234ABCD-...}, paste it below.
  7. Click Load league. The cookies are saved in your league file on this computer and never shown here again.

Cookies expire after a long time. If a saved league stops loading, remove it and add it again with fresh cookies.
